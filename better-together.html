This year has asked a lot of businesses and the people inside them.

So I’m setting aside two complimentary workshop slots for teams who could benefit from stepping away from the day-to-day, reconnecting, and experiencing a different way of working together.

The challenge

How might we bring out the best in how we work together as a team?

Teams can spend so much time getting the work done that they rarely get the opportunity to stop and look at how they work together.

This workshop creates that space.

Through a hands-on, facilitated experience, your team will explore what helps you work well together, surface different perspectives, and discover what could make working together even better.

The purpose

This is not traditional team building, and it’s not another workshop where a few people do most of the talking.

It’s an opportunity to:

  • Get everyone actively involved
  • Break away from slides and traditional discussion
  • Hear perspectives that may not normally surface
  • Create meaningful conversations and connection
  • See your team and how you work together from a different angle
  • Leave with insights you can take back into everyday work

And yes, have some fun along the way.

Why LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY®?

LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY® is a facilitated method that uses building, metaphor, storytelling, and reflection to help people think and communicate differently.

Instead of simply asking a question and discussing it, participants build their responses and share the meaning behind what they have created.

The result is a highly participatory experience where the conversation isn’t limited to the quickest thinker, the most senior person, or the loudest voice in the room.

Everyone builds. Everyone shares. Everyone contributes.

Who is it for?

This experience is designed for an existing team that works together regularly.

Leadership teams, startup teams, functional teams, project teams, or small organizations are all welcome to apply.

You don’t need to have a specific team problem to solve.

You simply need to be curious about how your team could work better together.
