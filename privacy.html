Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

What we collect

Information you choose to send us.

When you subscribe, contact us, or complete the WOW Assessment, we may collect your name, email address, company, role, WhatsApp number, message, assessment answers and results, and basic referral information.

How we use it

To respond and provide what you requested.

We use submitted information to reply to enquiries, deliver assessment results, manage newsletter subscriptions, understand interest in our services, and maintain appropriate business records. We do not sell personal information.

Who processes it

The services that make the website work.

We use Cloudflare to host and protect the website, HubSpot to manage form submissions and contacts, and Resend to deliver transactional emails. These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Analytics and cookies

Your choice, not a default.

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand page visits, engagement, and actions such as starting an assessment, subscribing, or requesting a consultation. Google Analytics loads only after you accept analytics cookies. You can decline or reopen Cookie choices from the footer at any time.

Retention and security

Only for as long as it is useful or required.

We retain information for the period needed to respond, provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask, correct, unsubscribe, or delete.

You may ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate business obligations.
