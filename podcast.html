What it is

An honest check-up for how your organization actually works.

Every episode, we sit down with leaders, practitioners and the occasional happy troublemaker to dig into how decisions really get made, how trust is built (or quietly destroyed), how power moves, and why your best people are exhausted by 2pm on a Tuesday.
